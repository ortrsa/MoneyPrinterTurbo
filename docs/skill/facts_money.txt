A 2019 study tested seventeen thousand lost wallets across forty countries. The more cash inside, the more likely people were to return it.
Paying with cash actually hurts. Brain scans show it activates the same region as physical pain - what psychologists call the pain of paying.
Credit cards numb that pain, so people spend more. One study found the average McDonald's order jumps from four fifty to seven dollars with a card.
Most prices end in ninety-nine cents because your brain reads the first digit, not the last. Nine ninety-nine just feels closer to nine than ten.
In late 2025, the US Mint struck its final penny after two hundred thirty-two years - each one cost nearly four times its own value to make.
Tipping was once considered so undemocratic that six US states outlawed it in the early nineteen-hundreds. Every one of those laws was gone within fifteen years.
