Vikings never actually wore helmets with horns - the image comes from a 19th-century opera costume designer, not real archaeology or any Viking-age artifact ever found
Most Vikings were ordinary farmers, fishermen and traders - raiding was more of a seasonal side job than a full-time career for most of them
Vikings bathed once a week without fail - it mattered so much that the Old Norse word for Saturday, laugardagr, literally means washing day
The average Viking man stood about five foot six - completely normal for Europe at the time, not a race of giants like the legends claim
Vikings reached North America roughly five hundred years before Columbus, landing on the coast of what is now Newfoundland
Viking women could legally own property and divorce their husbands - rights many other cultures denied women for centuries afterward
