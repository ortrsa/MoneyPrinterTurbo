SOURCES CHECKED:
- Multiple outlets (widely reproduced math, e.g. Vox "Cleopatra lived
  closer to the Moon landing than to the pyramids"): Great Pyramid
  completed c. 2560 BCE, Cleopatra VII died 30 BCE (gap ~2530 years),
  Moon landing 1969 CE (gap from Cleopatra ~1999 years) -- 1999 < 2530.
- Smithsonian / National Geographic / Zahi Hawass & Mark Lehner's Giza
  excavations: the pyramid workers' village (bakeries, breweries, a
  cemetery with proper burials, skeletons showing healed fractures
  consistent with medical care) -- evidence workers were paid laborers,
  not slaves.
- Britannica / Plutarch (Life of Antony): Cleopatra VII was a Macedonian
  Greek, descended from Ptolemy I Soter (one of Alexander the Great's
  generals); Plutarch specifically credits her as the first Ptolemaic
  ruler to bother learning the Egyptian language.
- Guinness World Records / architectural history: the Great Pyramid was
  the tallest human-made structure on Earth from c. 2560 BCE until
  Lincoln Cathedral's spire was completed in England in 1311 CE -- close
  to 3,800 years, hedged as "almost" since exact completion dates vary
  slightly by source.
- Ebers Papyrus (translated medical text) and multiple secondary sources
  (BBC, history-of-medicine surveys): ancient Egyptian physicians applied
  moldy bread to wounds. Hedged carefully in the script -- they did not
  understand germ theory or antibiotics; this is presented as a practice
  modern science later connected to real antimicrobial properties in
  mold, not as "they invented penicillin."
- A toothpaste recipe on a papyrus now in the Vienna collection (widely
  reported, e.g. a 2003 recreation by Austrian and Egyptian researchers):
  crushed rock salt, mint, dried iris flowers, and grains of pepper.
  Hedged as Greco-Roman-period Egypt (the papyrus is usually dated to
  around the 4th century AD, not the pharaonic Old/Middle/New Kingdom
  era) rather than implying every pharaoh brushed with this exact recipe.

VERIFIED FACTS:
- Toothpaste recipe on an Egyptian papyrus: rock salt, mint, dried iris,
  pepper -- genuine, though from the later Greco-Roman period of Egyptian
  history, not the pyramid-building era.
- Moldy bread used medicinally on wounds, documented in medical papyri,
  millennia before germ theory or penicillin.
- Pyramid workers were paid laborers with a supporting village and
  medical care, not slaves -- a real, well-evidenced myth-correction.
- The Great Pyramid held the "tallest human-made structure on Earth"
  record for roughly 3,800 years, until Lincoln Cathedral (1311 CE).
- Cleopatra VII was ethnically Macedonian Greek (Ptolemaic dynasty), not
  ethnically Egyptian, and is credited as the first in her dynasty to
  actually learn the Egyptian language.
- Cleopatra was born closer in time to the 1969 Moon landing (~1999
  years) than to the completion of the Great Pyramid (~2530 years).
