The word gymnasium comes from the Greek word for naked, because Olympic athletes actually competed completely nude.
Athens invented democracy, but only free adult men could vote. Women, slaves, and foreigners were excluded, roughly eighty percent of the population.
In Sparta, boys were taken from their families at age seven and raised by the state for brutal military training.
The Parthenon has almost no straight lines. Its columns curve slightly, likely to correct how they would otherwise look bent to the eye.
A two thousand year old bronze device called the Antikythera mechanism could predict eclipses. Nothing that complex existed again for fourteen hundred years.
Ancient doctors swore the Hippocratic oath, and modern versions of it are still used by medical schools today.
