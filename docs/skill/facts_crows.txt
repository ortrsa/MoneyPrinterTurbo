New Caledonian crows carve barbed hooks from twigs to fish insects out of tight spaces - one of the most advanced tool skills outside primates.
Researchers trapped wild crows wearing a caveman mask. Years later, the crows still scolded that mask - and had taught their chicks to do it too.
Crows gather around their own dead like a funeral - though scientists think it is less about grief and more about learning what killed one of their own.
Crows leave small gifts - buttons, earrings, scraps of metal - for people who feed them. Researchers suspect it is a learned reward loop, not gratitude.
On a puzzle where dropping stones raises water to reach a treat, crows solve it at roughly the level of a five to seven year old child.
You have probably heard crows love shiny objects. Controlled tests found no such preference - they grab dull objects just as often.
