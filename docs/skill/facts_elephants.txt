Elephant pregnancy lasts up to twenty-two months - the longest of any mammal on Earth.
An elephant's trunk has more than forty thousand individual muscles, and not a single bone.
Elephants can't jump. Their legs and ankles just aren't built for it - rhinos and hippos share the same limit.
In 2005, an elephant named Happy used a mirror to find a mark painted above her own eye - a real test of self-awareness.
Elephant matriarchs have led their herds to water sources they hadn't visited in thirty-five to forty-five years.
After a matriarch dies, researchers have documented elephants eating less, sleeping less, and returning to that spot for years.
