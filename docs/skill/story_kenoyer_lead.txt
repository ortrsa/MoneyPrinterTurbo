VERIFIED FACTS:
- Ina Thea Kenoyer, 48, of Minot, North Dakota. Steven Edward Riley Jr., 51, also
  of Minot, her boyfriend of about ten years.
- Riley believed he was due roughly $30 million from a "long-lost relative"
  inheritance, communicated to him by email. Kenoyer believed her decade with
  him entitled her to a share as his common-law wife -- North Dakota does not
  recognize common-law marriage, a detail investigators later noted.
- On September 3, 2023, Riley went to meet the "lawyer" handling the
  inheritance. He became violently ill shortly after and was hospitalized.
  He died September 5, 2023, at a hospital in Bismarck.
- Cause of death: ethylene glycol (antifreeze) poisoning. Investigators
  determined Kenoyer laced his drink (reported as sweet tea) with it.
- THE TWIST, the actual payoff of the story: investigators found no evidence
  the $30 million inheritance ever existed. Minot Police Chief John Klug said
  publicly: "To our knowledge, that $30 million doesn't exist. We have nothing
  to indicate that there was a long-lost relative." Prosecutors presented
  evidence the whole thing was an inheritance-scam email -- the lawyer was
  fictitious, the emails were a fraud scheme to extract personal information.
  Both Riley and Kenoyer apparently believed it was real.
- Kenoyer pleaded guilty to AA-felony murder in May 2024. Sentenced October 16,
  2024: 50 years, serving 25, with the remaining 25 suspended, followed by 10
  years of supervised probation.
