3	THE FACTS (independently verified):
	- Laika was a stray dog picked up off the streets of Moscow, originally named Kudryavka ("Little Curly"), nicknamed Laika ("Barker").
	- Launched aboard Sputnik 2 on November 3, 1957 -- the first living creature to orbit the Earth.
	- The spacecraft was a one-way mission: no re-entry/recovery system existed, so Laika was never going to survive the flight regardless of what actually killed her.
	- Official Soviet position, maintained for 45 years: she orbited peacefully for several days, then was painlessly euthanized via poisoned food before oxygen ran out.
	- In October 2002, Dr. Dimitri Malashenkov (a scientist on the original Sputnik 2 mission) presented findings at the World Space Congress revealing the true cause: a fault in the thermal control system caused the capsule's temperature to climb (multiple sources cite it reaching roughly 104°F), and Laika died from overheating and stress within hours of reaching orbit -- around the fourth orbit, not after several days.
	Sources: Wikipedia ("Laika"), Britannica, Space.com, History.com, SpaceDaily.
