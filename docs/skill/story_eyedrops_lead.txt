3	THE FACTS (all independently verified):
	- 2022-2023 US outbreak of VIM-GES-CRPA, an extensively drug-resistant strain of Pseudomonas aeruginosa never previously reported in the United States. Source: CDC Health Alert Network (HAN 00485), Wikipedia ("2022-2023 United States P. aeruginosa outbreak in eye drops").
	- As of the CDC's May 15, 2023 update: 81 patients identified across 18 states. 14 patients experienced vision loss; 4 patients died. Source: CDC, corroborated by WebMD, NPR, DrugWatch.
	- The product most consistently linked was EzriCare Artificial Tears (preservative-free, multidose bottles), manufactured by Global Pharma Health Care (India) and distributed/marketed by EzriCare LLC and Delsam Pharma. CDC lab testing found the outbreak strain in opened EzriCare bottles from multiple lots across different states.
	- FDA cited Global Pharma for current good manufacturing practice (cGMP) violations: lack of appropriate microbial testing, formulation issues, and lack of proper tamper-evident packaging controls. A nationwide voluntary recall of EzriCare and Delsam Pharma Artificial Tears followed.
