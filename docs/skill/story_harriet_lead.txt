3	THE FACTS (independently verified):
	- Harriet was a Galapagos tortoise who lived most of her later life at Australia Zoo (Queensland), owned by Steve and Terri Irwin, from 1987 until her death.
	- The popular/traditional story: Charles Darwin personally collected her as a small tortoise during his 1835 Galapagos visit on HMS Beagle.
	- She died June 23, 2006, of an acute heart attack/heart failure, at an age reported as 175 (one source says 176 -- HEDGE: exact age has a 1-year discrepancy across otherwise-reliable outlets, script uses "175" as the more commonly cited figure without claiming false precision).
	- DNA testing (1998) found Harriet is Chelonoidis niger porteri, a subspecies native to Santa Cruz Island in the Galapagos -- an island Darwin's own travel records show he never visited during the Beagle voyage.
	- Because of this species/location mismatch, most researchers/historians now doubt Darwin personally collected her, even though Australia Zoo's own official materials still tell the traditional Darwin-collected story.
	Sources: Snopes ("Did Charles Darwin and Steve Irwin Own the Same Tortoise?"), Wikipedia ("Harriet (tortoise)"), Australia Zoo's own history page, ABC News, Al Jazeera, CBC (death coverage, age reported as 175/176).
