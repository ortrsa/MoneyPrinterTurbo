The world's most expensive coffee is made from beans a civet cat already ate and pooped out. Buyers pay over a hundred dollars a pound for it.
Beethoven reportedly counted exactly sixty beans into every single cup, according to his own biographer - though scholars say there is no real proof he actually did it.
Decaf is not caffeine-free. Decaffeination only removes about ninety-seven percent, so every cup still has a few real milligrams in it.
Legend says an Ethiopian goat herder discovered coffee after his goats got too energetic to sleep from eating the berries - though historians think other people found it first.
Finland drinks more coffee per person than any other country on Earth, close to four cups a day for every single resident, including children.
You have probably heard coffee is the second most traded commodity on Earth after oil. It is not even close. By actual trade value, coffee ranks around ninety eighth.
