Antarctica is the largest desert on Earth, bigger than the Sahara, because deserts are defined by rainfall.
Ninety million years ago a temperate rainforest grew there, less than a thousand kilometres from the South Pole.
Blood Falls runs rust red because ancient iron rich brine oxidizes the moment it touches the air.
Mount Erebus, the world's southernmost active volcano, has churned a lava lake since at least nineteen seventy two.
